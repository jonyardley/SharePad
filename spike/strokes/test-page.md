# Stroke spike test page

One pass on the iPad, in this order. Tap **Snapshot** (top bar) at the end of each
section; the status line counts the snaps, so you can check you got 7.

Draw with the Apple Pencil throughout, in the white area, at 100% zoom until
section 7. Leave space between sections so they don't overlap.

## 1. Pen, pencil, marker

For each of pen, pencil and marker (pick it in the tool picker), draw three strokes:

- a light, slow wavy line
- a hard, fast wavy line
- one line that starts light and presses harder as it goes

For pencil, add a patch of shading with the Pencil tilted right over.

**Snapshot** (snap 1).

## 2. Monoline, fountain pen

Same three strokes for monoline and for fountain pen. With the fountain pen, also
write a word in your normal handwriting.

**Snapshot** (snap 2).

## 3. Watercolour, crayon

Same three strokes for watercolour and for crayon. Overlap two watercolour strokes
so the blending shows.

**Snapshot** (snap 3).

## 4. Colours and widths

With the pen: one stroke at the thinnest width, one at the thickest, one in a
light colour (yellow), one at reduced opacity if the picker offers it.

**Snapshot** (snap 4).

## 5. Eraser

Pixel eraser: rub through the middle of two strokes from section 1. Object
eraser: remove one whole stroke from section 2.

**Snapshot** (snap 5). From here the "rebuilt" column reads "no"; that is
expected, as the live points can't see erasing.

## 6. Lasso and undo

Lasso one group of strokes and drag it somewhere new. Then undo twice (three-finger
swipe left, or the undo arrow if the tool picker shows one) and redo once.

**Snapshot** (snap 6).

## 7. Zoom and crop

Pinch to zoom in to about 2x on the fountain pen handwriting, draw one stroke there,
then pan so only part of the page shows.

**Snapshot** (snap 7). The snapshot covers only what is on screen, so this tests
that the Mac crops and scales the same way.
