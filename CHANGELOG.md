# Changelog

User-facing release notes. **The topmost `##` section is embedded in the in-app
update dialog** (Sparkle shows it to people when they update), so write each entry
for users, not developers. Add a new `## <version>` section at the top before you
tag a release.

## 1.2.0
- After your trial, SharePad shows a live countdown in the window and the popover,
  so you know exactly when sharing will pause.
- You can now enter your licence straight from the message that appears when
  sharing pauses, so you don't have to go looking for it.
- After the trial, switching back and forth between two iPads no longer resets
  each one's sharing time.
- Clearer popover guidance while the iPad is connecting or waking, and gentler
  feedback if it disconnects mid-session.

## 1.1.0
- SharePad now has a 7-day free trial. After it ends, you can share for 5 minutes
  at a time before sharing pauses, until you add a licence: a one-time purchase
  (£6.99 at launch, now £2.99) that works offline, with no account.
- Already bought SharePad? You won't be charged again. Click the SharePad icon in
  your menu bar, choose "Enter Licence…", then "Lost your key?" to have your key
  emailed to you. Paste it in and the pause is gone for good.

## 1.0.6
- The in-app About panel now links to sharepad.co for buying and support, with
  quick "View Source" and "View Licence" links.
- Behind-the-scenes fixes. Nothing changes in how you use SharePad.

## 1.0.5
- If your iPad disconnects while you're sharing, SharePad now tells you: a
  brief alert appears in the menu-bar icon and the popover so you're not left
  wondering why your share went black.
- Privacy: only the iPad window can be picked in a video call's window
  picker. Other SharePad surfaces (the About panel, update dialogs) can't be
  shared by mistake.
- If the iPad stalls while connecting, SharePad now says so instead of looking
  live. If your organisation blocks camera access, SharePad explains that instead
  of showing a Settings button that can't help.

## 1.0.4
- Show or hide the share window from anywhere with a global keyboard shortcut
  (⌃⌥⌘H), no need to leave your call to reach the menu bar.
- Fix: the iPad now connects automatically the first time you plug it in, even
  while macOS is still settling the camera-permission and trust prompts.

## 1.0.3
- Maintenance release: security and code-signing updates behind the scenes. No
  changes to how SharePad works.

## 1.0.1
- Auto-update: SharePad now updates itself, and tells you what's new.

## 1.0.0
- First release. Plug your iPad into your Mac and it appears in a clean window,
  the same shape as the iPad, ready to share in any video call. No more setting up
  QuickTime every call.
